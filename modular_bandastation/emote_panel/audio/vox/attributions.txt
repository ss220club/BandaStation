The source code credits the individual authors above; sound/Attributions.txt
has no separate entry for these two files. They retain the source repository
CC BY-SA 3.0 asset declaration. Exact pinned paths/hashes for all five files
are recorded in source-assets.json alongside this attribution.

Vox quill/quills preserves the Paradise Vox feather-rustle sound and Russian
messages. Vox click/clicks adapts the authored Kidan click sound and changes
the message from jaws to a Vox beak. The Kidan clack echolocation/mineral scan
is not imported; playback uses the ordinary TG emote pipeline exactly once.

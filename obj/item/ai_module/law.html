law
/obj/item/ai_module/law

When slotted into an AI law rack, adds laws! The bread and butter of AI modules.

Vars

ionedIf TRUE, this board has sustained damage and must be repaired
lawsThis is where our laws get put at for the module
saved_lawsThe laws list last time save_laws() was called

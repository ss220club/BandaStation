Changes in PrimeDream: head.dmi includes the non-duplicate helmet.dmi states,
with head.dmi taking priority for general hats. helmet.dmi likewise includes
non-duplicate head states, retaining complete helmet artwork on name collisions.
Both files have aliases for TG hardhat, visor, surgical cap and head-mirror
names. mask.dmi and eyes.dmi also alias equivalent bandana and eyewear names
used by TG; no artwork is recoloured by the adapter.
mask.dmi retains the first occurrence of each state; subsequent duplicate human
mask sprites were removed. bodyparts.dmi adds vox_l_leg/vox_r_leg aliases from
the existing digitigrade leg sprites for the normal squashed-limb path.
Pixel art, frame directions, animation delays and authorship are preserved.
Other imported image files are unchanged, including nitrogen tank/mask items
and the added vox state in icons/mob/butts.dmi. Existing butt states are
pixel-identical to the PrimeDream base.

head.dmi and helmet.dmi add carved_pumpkin-emissive from the pinned raw helmet
states hardhat_pumpkin0/1. The mask keeps exact authored lit eye/mouth pixels
whose unlit RGBA is (43,22,3,255), (65,31,4,255) or (79,32,12,255), omitting
the recoloured pumpkin shell. All original states and pixels remain unchanged.
